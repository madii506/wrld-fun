02 · THE RULEBOOK

Up to 6 rules. Each one watches the world and spends a share of the coin’s chest (its claimed creator fees) when it fires. Start from a rulebook or write your own.

03 · THE FIRST BUY

pump.fun coins launch with a first buy. The tokens it buys are sent to your wallet right after the coin is created.

04 · LAUNCH

RULEBOOK

WHAT HAPPENS

1. we record the coin and its rulebook and give you its wallet
2. you send one SOL transfer to that wallet
3. the engine creates the coin on pump.fun from it, with your first buy
4. your tokens and any unused SOL come back to you
5. from then on the rules watch the world. Every fire is a public transaction on the coin’s page
