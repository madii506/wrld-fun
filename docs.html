wrld docs

5 min read · copy link
the uncomfortable part first. wrld is custodial. The engine holds each coin’s wallet, the wallet that pump.fun pays creator fees to, and it spends from it when rules fire. That is the only way a rule can act without someone signing. It is the same model rulr uses. Everything below is about what that wallet can and can’t do, and how to check it.

01overview

Every coin launched on wrld comes with a rulebook: up to six rules of the form when <the world does something> -> <the coin does something>. The world is read from public sources: prices, the weather, sports results and posts on X. The coin acts with its own money, the creator fees its trading produces.

02launching

  • You fill in the coin (name, ticker, picture), write the rulebook and choose a first buy.
  • wrld records it, derives the coin’s wallet and its mint address, and tells you one amount to send.
  • You send that one SOL transfer. No program approvals and no other signature.
  • The engine creates the coin on pump.fun from the coin’s wallet with your first buy, then sends the tokens and any unused SOL to your wallet.
  • From that moment the rules watch the world. Anything that happened before launch never counts.
first buyyour choice, 0.01–10 SOL (+2% for fees)
pump.fun rent and feesabout 0.03 SOL, the unused part comes back

03the language

Rules are stored as data and shown as one line each. The fingerprint is the first 12 hex characters of the SHA-256 of the rulebook’s canonical JSON. It is written into the coin’s pump.fun description at launch, so anyone can check the rulebook on the coin’s page against it.

05actions

  • buyback_burn(N% chest): buys the coin with N% of the chest and burns every token bought.
  • airdrop_holders(N% chest): buys the coin and sends the tokens to the top holders, pro rata.
  • pay_holders(N% chest): sends SOL to the top holders (up to 20), pro rata to their bags.
  • pay_creator(N% chest): sends SOL to the wallet that launched the coin.

Holders are the largest token accounts on-chain, minus the bonding curve, pools (program-owned accounts) and the coin’s own wallet. There is no sell action.

06when a rule fires

  • Levels (prices, weather, market cap) must be true on two reads in a row, at least 45 seconds apart. Then they fire once, and re-arm only after the level stops being true.
  • Events (a game result, a post, a time period) fire once per event, and only for events after launch.
  • Every rule has a cooldown: at most one fire per N hours (1–168).
  • A rule spends N% of the chest at the moment it fires. Below 0.002 SOL the fire is recorded but nothing is spent.
  • If the action fails, it is retried on the next passes, up to three times, and every try is logged.

07custody

Each coin’s wallet and mint keys are derived on the server from one master secret and the launch id, using HMAC-SHA256. They are never stored. The derived wallet is the coin’s pump.fun creator, so pump.fun pays creator fees to it.

  • The engine’s code has four spending actions, the four above, plus the launch hand-back, refunds and wrld’s 5%.
  • You can audit every one: the coin’s wallet is public, and each action appears in the coin’s log with its transaction.
  • What you are trusting: that the engine keeps running, and that the master secret stays secret. If either fails, rules stop firing; the fees stay in the coin’s wallet.

08fees and the chest

When at least 0.003 SOL of creator fees is waiting, the engine claims it into the coin’s wallet. wrld takes 5% of each claim. The rest, minus a 0.015 SOL gas reserve, is the chest that rules spend from. Swaps go through PumpPortal’s local transaction API, which adds its own 0.5% to each trade.
